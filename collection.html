The Collection

A year in four chapters.

Each QUATRÉA fragrance is composed from rare natural extracts, aged in glass for a full lunar cycle, and bottled by hand at our atelier in Grasse. Together, they form a single, slow portrait of the year.

№ 03 — The Architecture

A fragrance has three voices.

Every QUATRÉA perfume unfolds in three movements — a luminous opening, a beating heart, and a slow, lingering memory. Together they form a scent that lives for hours, and remembers for longer.

i. Prélude · Top Notes

The Opening

The first breath of a fragrance — bright, volatile, immediate. Lasts from the first minute to the first quarter hour.

  • — Bergamot
  • — Pink Pepper
  • — Pear Blossom
  • — Mandarin
ii. Cœur · Heart Notes

The Character

The soul of the perfume — the floral, spicy or vegetal signature that defines its identity over the following hours.

  • — White Peony
  • — Neroli
  • — Iris
  • — Vetiver
iii. Souvenir · Base Notes

The Memory

The slow, warm residue — woods, resins and ambers that linger on the skin long after the wearer has left the room.

  • — Cedarwood
  • — Black Amber
  • — Tobacco
  • — Oud · Musk

The Maison
"A fragrance is not made of essences alone — it is made of the season it remembers. We do not invent perfumes; we merely listen to the year, and give it a voice."

Each composition begins in the field — with a blossom, a leaf, a piece of bark — and ends in a hand-numbered bottle. We work in small batches, with patience as our only luxury.

Hélène Mercier Founder & Master Perfumer
