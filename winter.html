Nuit Éternelle

Black amber and frosted iris — the quiet elegance of a winter midnight. A scent that lingers like a held breath.

The Composition

A scent of held breath.

Nuit Éternelle opens with the cold, powdery breath of frosted iris, deepens into the resinous black of amber and labdanum, and settles — slowly, like snow — into the smoky, animalic depth of oud.

The Architecture

Three movements.

Nuit Éternelle unfolds like a long winter night — a cold, powdery opening, a resinous, liturgical heart, and the slow, smoky burn of oud as the fire finally settles.

i. Prélude · Top

The Opening

Cold, powdery, almost metallic — the first breath of frost on a stone wall at midnight.

  • — Frosted Iris
  • — Bergamot
  • — Carrot Seed
ii. Cœur · Heart

The Character

Black amber — resinous, sweet, almost liturgical. The deep, held centre of the perfume.

  • — Black Amber
  • — Labdanum
  • — Rose
iii. Souvenir · Base

The Memory

Smoky, animalic oud — the slow burn of incense, the residue of a long winter night.

  • — Oud
  • — Sandalwood
  • — Musk
The Inspiration
"Winter is not silence — it is the shape of silence. A held breath, a low fire, a long blue shadow. Nuit Éternelle is the scent of that shape."

Composed in the high Alps, where the air thins and the cold sharpens every note. Hélène Mercier ages the amber accord for a full lunar cycle before blending, allowing the resin to deepen into its black, honeyed heart.

Hélène Mercier Master Perfumer, Grasse
The Ritual

How to wear Nuit Éternelle.

i.

On bare skin

Apply to pulse points at the throat and wrists. The warmth of skin lifts the iris; the cold of the room deepens the amber.

ii.

On black fabric

Mist the inside of a black wool coat or silk lining. The oud sits against dark fabric like a shadow.

iii.

For the deepest night

Apply at midnight. The base notes — oud, sandalwood, musk — develop fully only in still, cold air.
