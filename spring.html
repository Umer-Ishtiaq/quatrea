Spring · Printemps

Fleur de Vie

A whisper of pear blossom and white peony — the first warm breath after winter's silence. Luminous, tender, alive.

The Composition

A scent of first light.

Fleur de Vie opens with the brittle sweetness of pear blossom, melts into the powdery heart of white peony, and settles — slowly, like the morning — into a soft, clean musk that lingers on the skin like a remembered kiss.

Concentration Eau de Parfum · 22%
Volumes 50 ml · 100 ml
Longevity 8 – 10 hours

The Architecture

Three movements.

Fleur de Vie unfolds like a slow morning — a luminous opening, a floral heart, and a clean memory that lingers on the skin long after the day has begun.

i. Top

The Opening

A burst of brittle, sun-warmed sweetness — the moment the blossom gives way to the morning.

The Memory

The Inspiration
"Spring does not arrive — it leaks in. A petal here, a warmth there, a breath of blossom on a still-cold wind. Fleur de Vie is the scent of that slow arrival."

Composed in the orchards of Provence, where the pear trees flower for only nine days each year. Hélène Mercier harvests the blossoms at dawn, before the bees wake, and carries them in linen sacks to the atelier in Grasse.

Hélène Mercier Master Perfumer, Grasse
The Ritual

How to use Fleur de Vie.

i.

On pulse points

Mist onto the wrists, the base of the throat, and behind the ears. Do not rub — let the warmth of the skin lift the notes slowly.

ii.

Layered, not alone

Fleur de Vie loves clean, unscented skin. Apply to freshly moisturised skin — the oils will carry the fragrance further into the day.

iii.

On fabric

For a softer, longer presence: mist the inside of a scarf or the lining of a coat. The musk lingers on linen for days.
