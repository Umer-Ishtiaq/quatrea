Summer · Été

Soleil d'Or

Sun-warmed fig leaf and golden neroli — the slow heat of a Mediterranean afternoon, distilled into a single drop.

The Composition

A scent of slow noon.

Soleil d'Or opens with the green, milky sweetness of fig leaf, warms into the honeyed glow of neroli, and settles into the dry, golden dust of cedar — the smell of a stone wall at five o'clock, still holding the day's heat.

The Architecture

Three movements.

Soleil d'Or unfolds like a long Mediterranean afternoon — a green and milky opening, a honeyed heart of neroli, and the dry, golden residue of cedar and amber as the sun begins to fall.

i. Prélude · Top

The Opening

Green, milky, almost lactonic — the cracked stem of a fig leaf held against the sun.

  • — Mandarin

The Character

iii. Souvenir · Base

The Memory

A dry, golden dust — cedar and ambrette, the residue of a long, hot afternoon on warm stone.

  • — Cedar

The Inspiration
"Summer is not a season — it is a temperature of memory. A wall still warm at midnight. A fig split open by the heat. Soleil d'Or is the scent of that remembered noon."

Composed on the Mediterranean coast, where the fig trees fruit twice and the heat presses down for weeks at a time. Hélène Mercier harvests the leaves in late July, before the sun is high, when they are still cool and damp with dew.

Hélène Mercier Master Perfumer, Grasse
The Ritual

How to wear Soleil d'Or.

i.

On warm skin

Apply after a warm shower, when the skin is still damp. The fig leaf opens with the heat and the neroli blooms within minutes.

ii.

In the hair

A single mist through the lengths of the hair. The cedar base clings to the strands and releases slowly through the day.

iii.

For the evening

Re-apply to the wrists before sunset. The dry-down — cedar and amber — sits beautifully against cool linen and warm skin.
